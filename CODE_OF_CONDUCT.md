# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in ServerPilot to be a straightforward, harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that keeps this project workable:

- Give and accept technical criticism about the work, not the person.
- Say what you actually observed and what you expected. A bug report with a
  reproduction is worth more than a strong opinion.
- Accept that a maintainer may decline a change. Scope is a real constraint.

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their explicit
  permission. This includes internal hostnames, cluster topology, usernames,
  and credentials that appear in logs or issue attachments.
- Sustained disruption of discussion.

## Scope

This applies in the project's repository, issues, pull requests, and
discussions, and whenever someone is representing the project in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through
[GitHub's private report flow](https://github.com/JinPLu/ServerPilot/security/advisories/new)
or by contacting the repository owner directly. Reports are handled
confidentially. The maintainers will respond as promptly as their availability
allows, and may warn, temporarily block, or permanently ban a participant.

Maintainers who do not follow this code in good faith may face the same
consequences as anyone else.
